Lifetime Customer Value

A customer relationship is rarely defined by one purchase. Its greater potential may develop through years of trust, useful support, repeat decisions, and shared success.

∞

Look beyond the immediate sale

Lifetime customer value is a way of viewing a customer according to the potential of the entire relationship—not simply the size of the opportunity in front of you today.

This perspective changes the question from “What can I sell right now?” to “What could we accomplish together over time?”
